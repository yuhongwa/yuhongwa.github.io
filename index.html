Projects

CropAI

CropAI (pronounced Cro-pAI, where "pAI" echoes Π, symbolizing mathematical and quantitative modeling; "Crop" spans cross-disciplinary and crop) explores modeling and computable problems in life sciences through a quantitative lens, toward multi-scale life design. We work at the intersection of genomics, multi-omics, virtual cells, and artificial plant life design.
